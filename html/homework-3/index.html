International Summit Reaches Climate Agreement

World leaders concluded a three-day climate summit in Geneva, announcing a joint agreement aimed at reducing global carbon emissions by 40% over the next decade. The agreement includes funding for renewable energy projects and stricter regulations on industrial pollution. Experts say the collaboration marks "significant progress" but warn that rapid action is needed to prevent irreversible environmental damage.

Historic Peace Talks Show Positive Signs

After years of tension, neighboring countries in Eastern Europe have resumed peace negotiations. Delegates from both sides described the meeting as "productive and hopeful." Officials expect a formal ceasefire proposal to be drafted by the end of next month.
