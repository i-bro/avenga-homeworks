Who We Are

Mini News Website is a simple educational project designed to demonstrate clean HTML structure, semantic tags like section and article, and multi-page layouts.

Our Mission

We aim to deliver clear, concise, and well-organized sample content that helps learners improve their web development skills through practical examples.

What You Will Learn

  • Creating semantic layouts using section and article
  • Building a basic login form
